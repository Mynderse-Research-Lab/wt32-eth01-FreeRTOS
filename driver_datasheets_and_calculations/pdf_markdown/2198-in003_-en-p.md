- Disconnect mains power before installation. 

- Verify that the rated voltage is compatible with the local supply voltage. 

- Connect the earth ground connection first.

## **Mounting Clearance Specifications** 

Minimum clearance on either side of the Bulletin 2198 AC line filters is 50 mm (1.97 in.). When line filter units are positioned side-by-side on the panel, only 50 mm (1.97 in.) minimum is required between them. 

**IMPORTANT** Minimize the wire length between te line filter and the input to the servo drive.
